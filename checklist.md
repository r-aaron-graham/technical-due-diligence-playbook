# Due Diligence Checklist

1. **Architecture**
   - Overall system design and modularity.
   - Technology stack and rationale for choices.
   - Scalability and performance characteristics.
2. **Security**
   - Authentication and authorization mechanisms.
   - Data encryption (at rest and in transit).
   - Vulnerability management and patching processes.
3. **Code Quality**
   - Test coverage and CI/CD practices.
   - Coding standards and documentation.
   - Dependency management and licensing.
4. **Operations**
   - Deployment processes and automation.
   - Monitoring, logging, and incident response.
   - Backup and disaster recovery.
5. **Team and Processes**
   - Team structure and expertise.
   - Development lifecycle and Agile practices.
   - Onboarding and knowledge sharing.
6. **Legal and Compliance**
   - Compliance with relevant regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, etc.).
   - IP ownership and licensing.
